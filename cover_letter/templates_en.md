## 💡 Cover Letter Writing Tips

### **Personalization Suggestions**
1. **Research Company Background** - Mention specific products or technologies
2. **Match Job Requirements** - Address keywords from the job description
3. **Demonstrate Business Value** - Use specific numbers to quantify your contributions
4. **Express Enthusiasm** - Explain why you're interested in this specific company/role

### **German Cover Letter Culture**
- **Formal Format** - Use proper salutations and closings
- **Concise and Clear** - Keep to one page, highlight key points
- **Professional Terminology** - Use industry-standard technical terms
- **Quantifiable Results** - German employers value specific performance metrics

### **Language Considerations**
- **English Proficiency** - Mention language certificates if English is not your native language
- **German Learning** - Express willingness and progress in learning German
- **Cultural Adaptation** - Demonstrate understanding of German work culture
